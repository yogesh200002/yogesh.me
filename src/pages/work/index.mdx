        # My Work
        
        Since entering the software industry in 2021, I've had the opportunity to work across startups, enterprise environments, and rapidly growing product teams. My experience spans frontend engineering, full-stack product development, developer experience, AI-powered applications, and platform architecture. I've contributed to products serving thousands of users, built systems that support critical business workflows, and helped take ideas from initial concepts to production-ready software.
        Below is a snapshot of the teams, products, and challenges I've worked on throughout my career.
        ***

        ## Frontend Developer
        *<a href="https://www.lucioai.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Lucio</a>*, March 2024 - Present

        - Founding frontend engineer on a legal-tech platform serving 3,000+ monthly active users across multiple countries and supporting $1M+ in annual revenue. Built and led a 4-person frontend team from the company's earliest stage.

        - Built the product's AI Assistant, including agentic workflow UI, multi-agent endpoint orchestration, conversation branching, and parallel response streaming. Architected a fault-tolerant streaming layer on Redis Streams and SSE with automatic session recovery so in-progress AI responses resume after network drops, tab closures, or device switches with no data loss.

        - Bootstrapped and led Lucio Studio, a cross-platform Electron desktop app with a Node.js + Hono backend and React frontend, from its first commit through roughly 30 shipped releases. Took the product from inception to beta while establishing shared architecture and tooling within a Turborepo monorepo. Since Clerk has no native support for Electron, engineered a custom auth implementation to bring unified authentication to the desktop app.

        - Migrated the core codebase from CRA to Vite, cutting production build times by 65% and getting HMR under 100ms. Profiled component re-renders and offloaded heavy compute to Web Workers, improving non-blocking UI performance by 50%.

        - Owned the backend for nearly every feature shipped on the frontend, building Node.js/Express/MongoDB services for auth, multi-tenancy, and Redis-backed streaming infrastructure. Operated full-stack across the platform rather than frontend only.
